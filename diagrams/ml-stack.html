Two things worth noticing about the shape of this

Only three models learn anything

GraphSAGE, cross-attention and LambdaMART are the only components trained on your data. Everything above them is pre-trained or deterministic — the same code runs identically for every client. Everything the system knows about your team's judgement lives in those three artefacts, and all three train on the same signal: which recommendations were accepted, edited, or dismissed.

That is also why the audit stage ships first. It needs none of them, and it produces the labels they need.

The removed layers were all generative

Three components were cut, and they have something in common: each one had a model inventing text rather than measuring something. Anchor generation invented phrases to insert into client copy. LLM-as-judge scored its own output. The reranker would have been a general model guessing at a task a purpose-trained one already handles.

What survived is measurement — embeddings, graph statistics, learned ranking over real labels. The one generative model left runs only when the answer is "this content doesn't exist yet."
